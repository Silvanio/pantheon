## Why

All three PDFs the system generates today (the Pedido de Compra per-supplier PDF, its consolidated summary, and the Daily Construction Report PDF) are plain, unbranded documents — no company identity, no indication of which project they belong to, and no record of who requested or approved the purchase they describe. Whoever prints or forwards these documents needs them to look professional and to be self-explanatory without the reader having to already know the context.

## What Changes

- Every PDF generated by `pantheon-service` SHALL open with a branded header: the company's logo (when one is configured) and name, and the construction site's ("obra"/project) name — sourced from the same `Company`/`ConstructionSite` data already used elsewhere, not duplicated or re-entered.
- The two Pedido de Compra PDFs (per-supplier and consolidated summary) additionally SHALL show, right under that header: who created the Pedido de Compra and when (its opening date), who approved each completed approval step (name and function, or "Equipe da empresa" when decided by company staff rather than a site member), and the Pedido de Compra's finalization date once concluded (or an "em andamento" indicator before that).
- General visual polish: consistent page margins, a header divider, and nicer table styling (header row background, alternating row shading) shared across all three PDFs via one common stylesheet fragment, instead of each service inlining its own near-duplicate `<style>` block.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
(none)

### Modified Capabilities
- `purchase-requests`: extends the "Per-supplier Pedido de Compra PDF" and "Pedido de Compra consolidated summary PDF" requirements with the branded header and the requester/approver/date metadata block.
- `daily-report-media-and-signoff`: extends the "PDF export" requirement with the branded header (company logo/name, project name).

## Impact

- `pantheon-service`: new shared `PdfBrandingService` (resolves company/site branding data and renders the header HTML/CSS fragment, reused by both existing PDF services); `PurchaseRequestPdfService` (new dependencies on `CompanyRepository`, `AppUserRepository`, `SiteMembershipRepository`, `PurchaseRequestApprovalRepository`, `PdfBrandingService`; new requester/approver metadata block); `DailyReportPdfService` (new dependency on `PdfBrandingService`).
- No API contract changes — same endpoints, same response shape, richer PDF content only.
