## Why

The current domain model conflates three unrelated things under `Project`: the paying tenant (company), the physical work site (`ConstructionSite`), and a generic team-membership list. In practice a company owns one or more construction sites ("obras"), and it is the *obra* — not the company as a whole — that has its own team (clients, service providers, architects, engineers), its own document projects, its own daily reports, and its own materials/budget workflow. Signup also skips a required commercial step: a company must choose a plan before it can use the product, and complete its legal/commercial profile (CNPJ, trade name, address, logo) before reaching the dashboard. Today, plan selection only appears reactively when a trial/plan expires, project creation bundles the company profile into one ad-hoc form, and there is no per-site permission control over what a given team member can do. This change corrects the entity model and the onboarding flow to match how the product is actually sold and used.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING**: `Project` is repurposed as `Company` — the top-level tenant a user signs up as. `ConstructionSite` ("Obra") moves to belong directly to `Company` and becomes the container for its own team, document projects, daily reports, equipment/material catalogs, and the materials/budget workflow (previously all scoped to `Project`).
- **BREAKING**: `ArchitecturalProject` is removed. In its place, a construction site owns one or more `Projetos` (site document projects): name, creation date, creating member, and one or more PDF attachments.
- **BREAKING**: Company-level `ProjectMembership` (with a construction function such as Client/Engineer/Architect/Site Foreman) is split in two:
  - `CompanyMembership` — internal company staff ("funcionários"), role `ADMIN`/`MEMBER`, invited the same way as today (pending registration until accepted).
  - `SiteMembership` — a construction site's own team: `CLIENT`, `ARCHITECT`, `ENGINEER`, `SITE_FOREMAN` (Mestre de Obra — invite required — existing account must accept, new account must register then accept), and `SERVICE_PROVIDER` with a trade sub-type (Pedreiro, Encanador, Eletricista, Terceirizado) for whom an account/login is optional.
- Signup flow reordered: account creation (email/password or Google) → **mandatory plan-selection modal** (Basic, Profissional, or Ilimitado; no billing yet) → company profile completion (Razão Social, Nome Fantasia, CNPJ, endereço, logo) → dashboard. The existing "create project or join project" onboarding popup and the combined project-registration form are removed; joining now happens exclusively by accepting a site invitation.
- Plans become a registered catalog (`Plan`: Basic, Profissional, Ilimitado) read from the database instead of a hardcoded three-way choice, each carrying a limit on the number of active construction sites (obras) a company may have (Basic: 2, Profissional: 10, Ilimitado: unlimited) — replacing today's project-count limit.
- Dashboard becomes a grid of cards, one per obra the company owns, each showing the obra's photo, name, and start date. The header gains a profile menu for changing the company's plan and editing its profile data.
- New per-obra permission management: the company can configure, per construction site, whether a given member (by site membership / construction function) can create vs. only view document projects and daily reports, and who can submit a material/budget request vs. approve one.
- The materials workflow (`material-request-workflow`) is extended into a full budget ("orçamento") flow: a request can now have one or more `Orcamento` (budget/quote) drafted against it, sent to the client, approved or rejected by the client, with payment-proof and invoice ("nota fiscal") attachments, in addition to the existing receipt-verification step (now also capturing delivery-proof photos).
- "Cronograma" is added as a visible, disabled/placeholder menu entry under the obra — explicitly not implemented yet.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `company-onboarding`: The signup-to-dashboard flow for a company — account creation, mandatory plan selection, company profile completion, and the dashboard/header surface for later editing the plan or profile.
- `company-plan-catalog`: The registered `Plan` catalog (Basic, Profissional, Ilimitado) and each plan's active-construction-site limit.
- `obra-team-management`: A construction site's own team — clients, service providers (optional account), architects, engineers, and site foremen (Mestre de Obra) — including the invite/accept lifecycle for the roles that require it.
- `site-document-projects`: `Projetos` owned by a construction site — name, creation date, creating member, and one-or-more PDF attachments.
- `obra-permission-management`: Per-construction-site, per-member (or per construction function) configuration of create-vs-view access to document projects and daily reports, and who may submit vs. approve a material/budget request.

### Modified Capabilities
- `pantheon-service`: `Project` becomes `Company`; company creation, trial, and plan-confirmation requirements are replaced by the new signup-order flow and the `Plan` catalog; `ConstructionSite` and the materials/reports capabilities move their ownership from `Project` to `Company`/`ConstructionSite` as described above; onboarding-status reporting reflects the new flow (plan pending vs. profile pending vs. ready).
- `construction-site-management`: `ConstructionSite` now belongs to a `Company`, not a `Project`; the `ArchitecturalProject` requirement and the company-wide "member construction function" / "list project members" requirements are removed (superseded by `site-document-projects` and `obra-team-management`); site creation/status update authority moves to the company administrator.
- `team-invitations`: Generalized to also drive obra-scoped invitations (client, architect, engineer), adds a CPF field for client invitations, and adds an optional (accountless) registration path for service providers.
- `daily-construction-report`: Rescoped from "member of a construction site's project" to "member of the construction site" (via `SiteMembership` or company staff), and creation is gated by the new per-obra permission configuration.
- `equipment-material-registry`: Rescoped from project administrator/`SITE_FOREMAN` to company administrator / obra roles, consistent with the new hierarchy.
- `material-request-workflow`: Extended with the `Orcamento` (budget/quote) sub-flow — creating a budget against a request, sending it to the client, client approval/rejection, payment-proof and invoice attachments — and the receipt-verification step gains delivery-proof photos.
- `pantheon-web`: The onboarding popup, project-registration view, and reactive plan-selection screen are replaced by the new signup → plan modal → company profile flow; the dashboard becomes an obra-card grid; a header profile menu is added for plan/company-profile edits; new views for obra team management, site document projects, the extended materials/budget flow, and per-obra permission configuration; "Cronograma" appears as a disabled placeholder menu entry.

## Impact

- **Affected code**: `pantheon-service` — `Project`/`ProjectMembership` entities, repositories, services, and controllers rename/split extensively (`Company`, `CompanyMembership`, `ConstructionSite` FK change, new `SiteMembership`, new `Plan`, `PlanSubscription`/company-plan link, new `SiteDocumentProject`, new `Orcamento`/budget entities, new `SitePermission` config); every controller/service currently keyed off "project admin" or "project member" is re-derived from `Company`/`ConstructionSite` membership. Extensive new Flyway migrations (renames, new tables, backfills) — full read/write/create/drop permission on the database has been granted for this.
- **Affected code**: `pantheon-web` — onboarding flow components/routes, dashboard, project (now company) registration/settings views, team panel (split into company staff vs. obra team), new document-projects and budget views, new permission-configuration view, i18n strings.
- **Affected code**: `pantheon-message` — invitation email templates extended for obra-scoped invitations (client/architect/engineer) and possibly a new "orçamento sent to client" notification.
- **Non-goals**: Cronograma implementation (menu placeholder only), billing/payment processing for plans, service-provider accounts beyond optional registration, multi-company administration UX beyond what already exists (a user can still administer more than one company; no new cap is introduced there), data migration/backfill *scripts* for any existing production data (this environment has none — migrations only need to support a clean slate).
